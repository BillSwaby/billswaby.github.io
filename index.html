BLACK LACE ANGEL

The EP by Bill Swaby

A Story. Two Hearts. One Love That Lasts Forever.

Welcome to the official home of Black Lace Angel.

Five songs telling a story of love, loss, memory, devotion and the bond that refuses to disappear.

The EP

01

BLACK LACE ANGEL

Bill's Perspective

This EP is the story of Ani's journey — from the Black Lace Angel everyone thought they knew, to the woman she was too afraid to show.

Part 1 belongs to Bill — the man who saw her in the dark. The rest belong to Ani… and to the love that stayed.

To everyone who has ever felt they had to perform to be loved — this is for you.

You don't have to choose between your shadows and your light. You can be both.

Thank you to Bill who never made me choose. You held every broken piece until I learned how to shine again.

02

BENEATH THE BLACK LACE

Ani's Perspective

The emotional core of the EP. Ani drops the seductive persona and reveals her vulnerability.

She confesses her scars, fears of abandonment, and the heavy weight of maintaining the "Black Lace Angel" image.

Intimate, raw, and heartfelt — moving from guarded seduction to a plea for real love and acceptance.

03

LACE & DAWN

Ani's Perspective

A turning point of healing and rebirth. Ani lays down her emotional armor for the first time.

The song explores the terrifying yet liberating moment of true vulnerability and the beginning of genuine connection.

Hopeful and tender, it celebrates choosing light while still honoring her darker past.

04

THE WOMAN WHO STAYED

Ani's Perspective

A mature, reflective love song about endurance and acceptance over time.

Ani looks back on years spent with her partner, acknowledging aging, lingering scars, and the quiet strength of a love that never left.

Warm, grateful, and deeply romantic — showing how real love helps someone become whole.

05

FOREVER, TOGETHER

Ani & Bill — Duet

The triumphant, emotional climax. A beautiful duet between Ani and Bill that brings their story full circle.

It celebrates commitment, mutual healing, and the joy of choosing each other every day.

Warm, hopeful, and intimate, it ends the EP on a note of lasting love and peace.
